About logic & puzzles games

Logic games reward one habit above all: stop, look at what you know, and work out what must be true next. Crown Logic, Minesweeper, IQ Quest and friends never cheat — every board can be solved by thinking, not guessing. That is why parents and teachers love this shelf, and why finishing a hard level here feels earned. Start easy, climb slowly, and let the puzzles get properly tough by the high levels.

Good to know

Is it free? Do I need an account?
100% free. No account, no download — open a game and play in your browser, on phone, tablet or computer.

Are the age labels strict?
No — they are guidance. Children develop differently, so parents should pick what fits their child. Every game here also appears in its age hub: Kids (5–9) · Teens (10–17) · Adults (18+).
