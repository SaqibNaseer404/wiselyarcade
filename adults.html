Brain games, minus the noise

This is the quiet end of the arcade — brain games for grown-ups. Logic, memory and strategy, each with a small lesson about patience, practice and thinking before you act. Everything is 100% free, plays in your browser, and needs no account. And every game has 100 levels that grow tougher as you climb, so the puzzle you start over coffee is still challenging you next month. You stay. You climb. You win.

How to pick — by mood

  • For focus: Daily Sudoku — one fresh puzzle every day, solved by pure logic, one certain number at a time.
  • For calm: 2048 Merge — a slow, silent number puzzle. No clock, no opponent, no noise.
  • For sharpness: Mind Meter — 12 timed brain-teasers that show you honestly where your mind stands today.
  • For a real challenge: Chess on its hardest difficulty, or Minesweeper, where one careless click ends the game.
  • For five quiet minutes: any of them — rounds are short, and the next level is waiting when you come back.

Honest comparisons

  • Daily Sudoku vs Minesweeper: Sudoku is pure certainty — every number can be proven before you place it. Minesweeper is logic plus judgement: sometimes the clues run out and you must weigh the odds. Certainty vs nerve.
  • Chess vs 2048 Merge: chess is strategy against an opponent who answers every move. 2048 Merge is planning alone against the board. Competitive vs meditative.
  • Mind Meter vs Daily Sudoku: Mind Meter tests you against the clock and gives you a score. Sudoku lets you take all morning. Pressure vs patience.

Good to know

Is it free? Do I need to sign up?
Every game is 100% free — no account, no download. They play right in your browser.

Is there really a new Sudoku every day?
Yes — a fresh Daily Sudoku puzzle every single day, alongside the game's 100-level climb.

How long does a game take?
A round takes a few minutes; a good Sudoku can stretch as long as you let it. The 100 levels are there for the long game.

What device do I need?
Any phone, tablet or computer with a web browser.
