Find your game

This is your hub — faster, trickier games for ages 10 to 17. Everything here is 100% free, plays in your browser with no account, and every game has 100 levels that keep getting harder the higher you climb. Each game also carries a life lesson — not the preachy kind, just the true kind: greed traps you, balance beats speed, think before you move. You stay. You climb. You win.

How to pick

  • You live for speed: Apex GP (motorcycles), Nitro GP (cars) or Sky GP (flying cars, in a bright daytime sky) — real 3D Grand Prix racing, 3 laps against 5 rivals, with a garage where your points buy upgrades.
  • You prefer tricks to laps: Trail Stunts — throttle, tilt and flip a dirt bike over wild hills, and stick the landing.
  • Classic arcade reflexes: Neon Snake and Tower Stack — both take ten seconds to learn and get genuinely brutal by the high levels.
  • Brain over thumbs: Chess (three difficulty levels) and 2048 Merge — pure planning, no luck.
  • Puzzles and proof: IQ Quest — patterns, sequences, codes and riddles, with the reason explained after every solve — and GK Quest, 100 levels of general knowledge where every answer teaches a true fact.
  • Maths that doesn't feel like homework: Math Quest — tables to 12, missing numbers and two-step problems at Teen, Adults and Expert levels.
  • You want a score that judges you: Mind Meter — 12 timed brain-teasers and one honest meter.
  • Only got two minutes: Forest Dash or Bubble Pop — jump straight in, no setup.

Honest comparisons

  • Apex GP vs Nitro GP vs Sky GP: same Grand Prix structure — 3 laps, 5 rivals, career levels, garage upgrades. The split is pure taste: bikes lean and weave, cars drift and muscle, and Sky GP takes the same formula into the sky — golden rings to thread, bird flocks to dodge, and a Time Trial mode the other two don't have.
  • Chess vs Mind Meter: chess is slow, deep strategy against an opponent who answers back. Mind Meter is your brain against the clock. One rewards patience; the other rewards speed of thought.
  • Neon Snake vs Tower Stack: Snake punishes greed — the longer you grow, the easier you trap yourself. Tower Stack punishes rushing — one hurried drop ruins the whole tower. Reflexes vs timing.

Quick answers

Is it free? Do I need an account?
100% free. No account, no download — open a game and play in your browser.

Can I play on my phone?
Yes. The games run in the browser on phone, tablet or computer.

How do the 100 levels work?
Finish a level to unlock the next. Levels get tougher as you climb — faster, trickier, less room for error. Clearing all 100 means you have genuinely mastered the game.

How long does a game take?
Most rounds take a few minutes. The full 100-level climb is a longer project — days of short sessions. That is kind of the point.
