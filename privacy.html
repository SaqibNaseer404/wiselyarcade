Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Wisely Arcade ("we", "our") is designed to be safe for the whole family. This policy explains, in plain language, what we do — and don't do — with your information.

The short version

We collect nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no newsletters, and no tracking of who you are. You can play every game on this site without telling us anything about yourself.

High scores stay on your device

When you set a best score or best time in a game, it is saved using your browser's localStorage — a small storage space inside your own browser on your own device. That data never leaves your device, is never sent to us, and can be erased at any time by clearing your browser data.

Children's privacy

Wisely Arcade welcomes players of all ages, including children. Because we collect no personal data at all, no personal data from children is stored anywhere — not on our servers (we don't run any), and not by us in any form. There is no way for a child to share personal information with us through normal play, because the site never asks for any.

Cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. If third-party advertising (such as Google AdSense) is shown on the site in the future, that provider may use cookies as described in its own privacy policy.
