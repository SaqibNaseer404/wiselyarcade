About maths & numbers games

Number confidence grows the same way game skill does: small wins, repeated daily. Math Quest drills the four sums in stages from Toddler to Expert, Daily Sudoku teaches patient logic one cell at a time, and 2048 Merge turns planning with numbers into a chase for one legendary tile. None of it feels like a worksheet — but the practice is real, and it adds up.

Good to know

Is it free? Do I need an account?
100% free. No account, no download — open a game and play in your browser, on phone, tablet or computer.

Are the age labels strict?
No — they are guidance. Children develop differently, so parents should pick what fits their child. Every game here also appears in its age hub: Kids (5–9) · Teens (10–17) · Adults (18+).
