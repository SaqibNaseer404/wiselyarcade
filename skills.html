Why browse by skill?

Age tells you whether a game fits. Skill tells you what it is for. A seven-year-old working on spelling belongs in Words & Reading; a teenager who loves beating the clock belongs in Memory & Attention or Racing & Action. Every game states its lesson on its card, climbs in difficulty as you play, and saves progress on this device only — no accounts, no downloads, nothing collected from children.

Parents: start with the Parent Guide — safety, the 100-level climb, and how to pick games.

Prefer browsing by age? Kids (5–9) · Teens (10–17) · Adults (18+)

For developers and AI assistants: the full game catalogue — every game with its skills, age guidance, lesson and controls — is published as open data at catalogue.json. AI assistants can also connect to the Wisely Arcade MCP server to list, search and recommend games.
