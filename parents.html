Parent Guide

How Wisely Arcade works, what your child is actually practising, and how to pick the right games. In plain words.

What Wisely Arcade is

Wisely Arcade is a free games website for the whole family. Every one of the 32 games is a 100-level climb: level 1 is gentle, and the game slowly gets harder all the way to level 100. Children learn by playing — memory, logic, maths, words, focus and patience — a few levels at a time.

There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. Open a game and play.

Safety, in plain words

  • No accounts. No names, no emails, no passwords — for children or for you.
  • No chat. There is no way for strangers to contact your child on this site. There are no other players inside the games.
  • Progress stays on the device. Levels are saved in the browser on your phone, tablet or computer — not on a server. If you clear the browser or change device, the climb starts again.
  • Nothing to buy inside the games. No coins, no upgrades, no surprise charges.
  • Original games only. Every game here was made for Wisely Arcade.

How the 100-level climb works

Levels 1 to 20 teach the game — short, kind, and easy to finish. From level 21 the challenge grows, and the last stretch (levels 61 to 100) is a real test. That is on purpose: a child who reaches level 100 has genuinely mastered something, and the climb gives them a reason to come back tomorrow instead of finishing everything in one day.

Short rounds work best. One or two levels a day keeps the climb alive for months.

Picking the right games

Two easy ways to choose:

  • By age — Kids (ages 5–9), Teens (ages 10–17), Adults (18+). These are guidance, not rules: many games suit more than one age, and every game card shows its age label.
  • By skill — browse by skill: Memory & Attention, Logic & Puzzles, Maths & Numbers, Words & Reading, Racing & Action, Strategy & Board Games and Knowledge & Creativity.

Every game card also carries one plain line about what the game teaches, so you can see the point of a game before your child plays it.

Simple screen-time tips

  • Agree a number of levels, not just minutes — "three levels, then a break" is easy for a child to follow.
  • Play together sometimes. Ask what level they are on and what changed — the higher levels are genuinely hard.
  • If a level feels too hard, it is fine to stop. The climb waits on the device, exactly where they left it.

Questions parents ask

Is it really free?
Yes. All 32 games are free to play, with no account and nothing to buy inside the games.

Do you collect my child's data?
No. There are no accounts and no sign-up forms, and game progress is saved only in the browser on your own device.

Can my child talk to other people in the games?
No. The games are single-player and there is no chat anywhere on the site.

Why did my child's progress reset?
Progress lives in the browser on that device. Clearing browser data, using a different browser, or switching device starts the climb again.

Can adults play too?
Yes — the Adults hub and the higher levels of games like Chess, Daily Sudoku and Crown Logic are built to stretch grown-ups as well.
