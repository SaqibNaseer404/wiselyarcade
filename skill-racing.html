About racing & action games

This is the loud shelf: Grand Prix bikes, cars and flying cars, endless runners, stunt bikes and precision platform jumps. But look at the lessons — smooth is fast, balance beats speed, falling teaches timing. These games reward the calm player, not the frantic one, and every racer climbs 100 career levels with upgrades earned along the way. Tilt steering works on phones, and every game plays in the browser with no download.

Good to know

Is it free? Do I need an account?
100% free. No account, no download — open a game and play in your browser, on phone, tablet or computer.

Are the age labels strict?
No — they are guidance. Children develop differently, so parents should pick what fits their child. Every game here also appears in its age hub: Kids (5–9) · Teens (10–17) · Adults (18+).
