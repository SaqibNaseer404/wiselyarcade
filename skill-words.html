About words & reading games

Every strong reader started with single letters. Missing Letters begins there — one picture, one missing letter — and the path climbs through Letter Garden, Word Pet and Word Town, where each finished word grows a plant, feeds a puppy, or builds a piece of a 3D town. Daily Word Guess keeps older players honest with a fresh word every day. Short rounds, kind feedback, and real spelling practice in every level.

Good to know

Is it free? Do I need an account?
100% free. No account, no download — open a game and play in your browser, on phone, tablet or computer.

Are the age labels strict?
No — they are guidance. Children develop differently, so parents should pick what fits their child. Every game here also appears in its age hub: Kids (5–9) · Teens (10–17) · Adults (18+).
