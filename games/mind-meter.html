How to Play

Mind Meter is a race against your own brain: 12 original puzzles drawn from number logic, visual patterns, word reasoning, and classic math riddles. Each question gives you 30 seconds on the clock.

  1. Read the question, pick one of the four answers — faster answers earn bigger time bonuses.
  2. Chain correct answers into a streak — every streak step adds bonus points, up to +100 per question.
  3. Run out of time and the question is gone — no points, streak resets. Keep moving!
  4. At the end you get an estimated IQ score (85–145 scale) and a rank, from Curious Mind up to Certified Genius. Beat your best next time.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap an answer. Move your mouse or tilt your phone — the 3D background drifts with you.
  • Keyboard: press 1–4 to answer A–D.

The LessonA sharp mind is built, not born

Every IQ question you solve is a rep for your brain — the same way every push-up is a rep for your muscles. Nobody is born a genius at puzzles; the people who ace them have simply done hundreds before you.

So a low score today means nothing except "you haven't practiced yet." Play again tomorrow, and the meter moves. Brains grow the same way everything else grows: one small effort, repeated.
