How to Play

  1. Look at the big picture above the empty boxes. What is it?
  2. Tap the letter tiles in the right order to build the word — the letters are all there, just shuffled.
  3. Every word you finish builds a new piece of your 3D town — a house, a shop, a little park, a school, a tower. Watch it rise!
  4. There are 100 levels, with 6 words in each. Words start tiny (CAT, HOME) and grow longer as you climb. From Level 21, two cheeky trap letters sneak into the tiles; from Level 51, the picture hides until you place the first letter; and from Level 81, there is no picture at all — the finished word reveals it!
  5. Your town keeps every level you finish, so it grows and grows. Finish Level 100 and a golden tower shines over your complete town. Finish a level to unlock the next.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap or click the letter tiles in order.
  • Wrong tile? No problem — it shakes, and you can try another. Trying is how we learn. A star is earned for each word built with no wrong taps.
  • Town view: your town slowly turns on its own, so you can admire it from every side while you play.

The LessonGreat towns are built one brick — and one word — at a time

No town appears in a single day. First one house, then a street, then a school — brick by brick, each small piece placed on top of the last. Learning works exactly the same way: one letter, then one word, then one whole town of words you can read and build with.

So when something big feels too big, remember your town. You never have to build it all at once. Place one brick — learn one word — today, and the next one tomorrow. Step by small step, the empty field becomes a town, and the beginner becomes a builder.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play Word Town?

Look at the picture, then tap the shuffled letter tiles in the right order to complete the word. Every finished word builds a new piece of your 3D town — a house, a shop, a park, a school or a tower.

How many levels are there?

There are 100 levels, with 6 words in each level. Words start tiny, like CAT and HOME, and grow longer as you climb — all the way to long words like SUPERMARKET and SKYSCRAPER. Your town keeps growing with every level you finish.

What happens if I tap the wrong tile?

No problem — the tile shakes and you can try another one. Nothing is taken away. A star is earned for each word you build with no wrong taps.

Who is Word Town for?

It is made for young readers and builders who are learning to spell everyday words about homes, schools and towns. The picture gives a clue for almost every word, so early readers can play on their own.

What does Word Town teach?

It teaches that great towns are built one brick — and one word — at a time. One small step, placed on top of the last, is how every big thing gets built.
