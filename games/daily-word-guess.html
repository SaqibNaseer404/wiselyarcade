How to Play

  1. 📅 Today's Word: one hidden 5-letter word per day — the same word for everyone. Guess it in 6 tries. Win days in a row to build your 🔥 streak, and come back tomorrow for a new word.
  2. Type letters with the on-screen keyboard (or your real keyboard) and press Enter to guess.
  3. After each guess the tiles flip: green = right letter, right place · gold = right letter, wrong place · grey = not in the word at all. Watch out — a letter can appear twice!
  4. 🧗 Level Climb: 100 levels that grow with you. Levels 1–25 use 4-letter words (5 tries), 26–50 use 5 letters, 51–75 use 6 letters, and 76–100 use mighty 7-letter words. Finish a word quickly to earn up to ⭐⭐⭐ per level.
  5. Any word of the right length counts as a guess — so think out loud, try bold guesses, and let the colours teach you.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap the letter keys, ⌫ deletes and Enter guesses.
  • Keyboard: type letters, Backspace to delete, Enter to guess.

The LessonNever give up — every wrong guess teaches you something

A wrong guess in this game is never wasted. A grey letter crosses out a dead end. A gold letter whispers "I'm in the word — just not here." Every try, even the ones that miss, hands you a clue you did not have before.

Life works exactly the same way. The attempt that "fails" is quietly teaching you where the answer isn't — and narrowing down where it is. The only true loss is the guess you never make. So keep guessing, keep learning, and never give up: the word is always findable, one honest try at a time.
