How to Play

Apex GP is real 3D motorcycle racing, right in your browser. You start at the back of a 6-bike grid — fight your way to the front over 3 laps. Brake before corners, lean through them, and fire the nitro on the straights.

  1. 10 career levels. Level 1 is the classic 3-lap circuit; levels 2–10 are straight daylight sprints — point-to-point, no laps. Finish in the top 3 to unlock the next level.
  2. Earn points for where you finish — 1st place pays 100. Points never expire.
  3. Upgrade your bike in the garage: Engine (top speed), Tires (grip in corners), Nitro (boost power). Five stages each — a fully upgraded bike is a different animal.
  4. Sprint rivers: every river has a ramp just before it. Hit it fast — around 120 km/h or more — or you'll splash down and have to retry the jump. Slow is wet!
  5. Stay on the asphalt. The grass slows you down and the walls scrub your speed — smooth is fast.

Controls

  • Keyboard: ↑/W throttle · ↓/S brake · ← →/AD steer · Shift nitro.
  • Touch: ◀ ▶ to steer, ▲ throttle, ▼ brake, N₂O for nitro.

The LessonSmooth is fast — control beats raw speed

In Apex GP, the rider who pins the throttle everywhere doesn't win — they run wide, hit the grass, and kiss the wall. The winner brakes early, leans clean through the corner, and fires the nitro exactly where it counts. Raw speed without control is just a faster way to crash.

Life works the same way. Rushing every decision, answering every message instantly, sprinting without a plan — that's pinning the throttle everywhere. The people who go far aren't the fastest starters; they're the ones who stay smooth when things get curvy. Control your speed, pick your moments, and you'll beat raw speed every time.
