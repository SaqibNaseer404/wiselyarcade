How to Play

  1. Look at the big picture. What is it?
  2. Read the word below it — one letter is missing, shown as a yellow tile.
  3. Tap the letter, from the three big buttons, that finishes the word.
  4. Pick your level at the top: Toddler (tiny words), Kids, Teen, Adults, up to Expert — two missing letters in long words!
  5. Finish all the words. A star is earned when you find the letter on your first try — how many can you collect?

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap or click one of the three letter buttons.
  • Wrong letter? No problem — the tile shakes, and you can try again. Trying is how we learn.

The LessonReading grows one letter at a time

Nobody learns to read in one giant jump. We learn one letter, then one word, then one sentence — small step by small step, exactly like this game. A missing letter is not a wall; it is a puzzle waiting for you to solve it.

That is how every big thing in life gets learned: one small step, then the next. So when something feels too hard, don't try to swallow it whole. Find the one missing letter — the one small step — and take that. Then the next one. Soon you'll look back and find you can read the whole page.
