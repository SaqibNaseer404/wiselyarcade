How to Play

  1. Press Start Fight and face your rival across the arena. Beat them before the 60-second timer runs out — if time ends, the fighter with more health left wins.
  2. Move with A / D, jump with W, and strike with J (punch) and K (kick). Kicks are slower but reach further and hit harder.
  3. Hold S to block. Blocked hits barely scratch you — and every block fills your blue Calm meter.
  4. At 50 Calm, press L for the Focus Strike: a dashing blow that deals huge damage. Calm is power — the fighter who keeps their cool wins.
  5. Win to earn up to 3 stars (finish with high health) and unlock the next rival. There are 100 levels: rivals return stronger with new Dans, champions wait every 10 levels, and the Level 100 rival is… your own anger.

Controls

  • Move: A / D or ← / →
  • Jump: W or ↑ (attack in the air for a flying knee)
  • Block: hold S or ↓ — blocking builds Calm
  • Punch: J or F · Kick: K or G · Focus Strike: L or H (needs 50 Calm)
  • Touch screens: use the on-screen buttons under the arena.

The LessonReal strength is self-control

Every martial art teaches the same secret: the fighter who loses their temper loses the fight. Anger makes you swing wild and leave yourself open. Calm makes you watch, breathe, block — and strike once, cleanly, at exactly the right moment. That is why blocking builds your power in this arena: staying cool is not passive, it is how champions charge up.

It works far beyond the arena. In an argument, in traffic, in a hard exam — the person who keeps their calm keeps their power. So breathe out, hold your guard, and choose your moment. Strength that cannot control itself is not strength at all.
