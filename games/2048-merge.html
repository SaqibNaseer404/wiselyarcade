How to Play

  1. Slide all the tiles with your arrow keys or a swipe. Every tile moves as far as it can go.
  2. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one tile worth double.
  3. Every merge adds to your score, and a fresh tile pops in after each move.
  4. Reach the 2048 tile to win — then keep going for an even bigger score.
  5. The board fills fast, so plan ahead. When no moves remain, the game ends.

Controls

  • Keyboard: ← ↑ ↓ → or W A S D to slide the tiles.
  • Touch: swipe up, down, left, or right on the board.

The LessonSmall smart steps add up to big wins

Every 2048 tile starts as a humble little 2. Nobody wins by chasing the big number directly — winners patiently pair small tiles, again and again, until the small wins combine into something huge.

That's compounding, and it works everywhere: study a little each day and knowledge doubles, save a little each month and money grows, show up kindly every day and trust builds. Don't hunt the 2048 moment — make the next small merge.
