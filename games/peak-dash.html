How to Play

  1. Run and jump across the mountain platforms and reach the pink summit flag to finish the level.
  2. Coins sparkle along the way — grab as many as you can. They are a bonus, never a requirement.
  3. Spikes and long falls cost you 1 heart. You pop back to the last safe ground you stood on. Lose all 3 hearts and the level ends — just try again.
  4. New tricks arrive one at a time: spike patches first, then moving platforms that ferry you over wide pits, then springs that bounce you to high ledges.
  5. Stars: finish with no falls for 3 stars, one fall for 2 stars, two falls for 1 star. (A third fall ends the level, so a finished level always earns at least one star.)
  6. There are 100 levels in 10 worlds, from Sunny Slopes to The Grand Peak. Jumps get longer, climbs get higher and pits get wider as you rise — and every level is test-played by a robot climber before it ships, so every summit can always be reached. Your progress and stars save on your device.

Controls

  • Keyboard: run with ← / → or A / D. Jump with Space, W or ↑.
  • Touch: on phones and tablets, use the on-screen ◀ ▶ buttons to run and the big ⬆ button to jump.
  • Friendly jumping: the jump is forgiving — press a moment too early or a moment too late at a ledge edge and the climber still jumps. Run at a gap at full speed and leap from the very edge for your longest jumps.

The LessonFalling is information.

Every failed jump teaches you the timing: where the edge really is, how long to run before you leap, when to wait for the platform. A fall is not the opposite of progress — it is how the mountain explains itself. Mastery is just failing smarter, faster, until the failures run out.

It works the same way off the mountain. A missed answer in class, a cake that flops, a song that falls apart — each one is data with a bruise on it. The climbers who reach summits are not the ones who never fall. They are the ones who read every fall and adjust the next jump.
