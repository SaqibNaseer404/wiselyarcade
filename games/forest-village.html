Forest Village

Run a real 3D forest village. Every morning, assign your villagers — gather food, chop wood, build shelter. Every night, the village eats, the fire burns, and the weather tests your plan. Learn real survival skills — making fire by rubbing sticks, sparking flint, even a battery-and-wires trick — to solve real problems. 100 days, tough from Day 1.

How to Play

  1. Pick a day. Read the weather, the goal, and tonight's costs — the meal and the fire.
  2. Assign every villager a job with the + / − buttons: 🧺 gather food, 🪓 chop wood, 🔨 build. Watch them work in real 3D — drag the view to look around your village.
  3. On skill days, a real problem appears — the fire is out, the water is unsafe, the shelter is falling. Learn the real survival skill, answer the question, and put the steps in order. Solve it with no mistakes to earn the bonus.
  4. Press Start the day. At nightfall you see the full maths: what came in, what the meal and fire cost, and what is left.
  5. Hit the target with the village safe to earn stars and unlock the next day. There are 100 days — and this game is tough from Day 1. There is no easy warm-up. Plan backwards from tonight.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap + and − to assign villagers, tap answers and steps.
  • Look around: drag the 3D village left or right to turn the camera.
  • Failed a day? The night summary shows every number. Change the plan, not the goal — then try again.

The LessonPlan backwards: tonight decides today

Every real village, camp and household runs on the same rule Forest Village teaches: tonight's costs are fixed — today's work must cover them. The meal will be eaten. The fire will burn. The storm will not wait. So wise planners do not ask "what shall we do today?" They ask "what must be true by tonight?" — and work backwards from there.

That is budgeting, in its oldest form. Food in, food out. Wood in, wood out. Hands are limited, so every choice costs another choice: a builder cannot gather, a gatherer cannot chop. And woven through the days are real survival skills — friction fire, flint sparks, boiling water, lashings, finding north by shadow — taught the way they are used in life: as answers to problems, exactly when the problem bites.

About This Game

Forest Village trains planning, budgeting maths and real-world problem solving in a real-time 3D world you can turn and explore. It is made for ages 7 and up: children who love building and strategy, teens, and adults who enjoy a hard planning puzzle. The survival skills taught are real, safe-to-know knowledge — the battery fire lesson includes its safety rule, and the game always teaches adult supervision for real fire. Progress is saved on your device only — no account, no sign-up.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play Forest Village?

Each day, assign your villagers to gather food, chop wood or build. At night the village eats a set amount of food and the fire burns wood. End the night with the target stock still in store, and keep everyone fed, warm and sheltered to pass the day.

How many levels are there?

There are 100 days. The village grows from 5 villagers to 14, the weather turns harsher, and the targets keep rising. The game is tough from Day 1 by design — Day 1 already has one best plan.

Are the survival skills in the game real?

Yes. Friction fire (rubbing sticks), flint and steel sparks, the battery-and-steel-wool method, boiling water to kill germs, lashings, the shadow-stick compass and the rule of three for rescue signals are all real techniques, taught step by step. Real fire should only ever be practised with an adult.

Is Forest Village really 3D?

Yes — the village is a real 3D scene. Your villagers walk to their jobs, tents and huts appear as you build them, rain falls on wet days, and the sky darkens at nightfall. Drag the view to look around.

Who is Forest Village for?

It is made for ages 7 and up — kids who like building and strategy games, teens, and adults. It teaches planning and budgeting maths through play, plus genuine outdoor know-how.
