How to Play

The classic logic puzzle everyone knows — now with juicy 3D tiles. Somewhere under the board, mines are hiding. Your job: reveal every safe tile without touching a single one.

  1. Tap or click a tile to reveal it. A number tells you exactly how many mines are touching that tile — use the numbers to deduce where the mines must be.
  2. Reveal an empty tile and the whole safe area opens up in a flood. That's your reward for a brave first click — which is always safe.
  3. Sure a tile hides a mine? Long-press it (or right-click on desktop) to plant a flag. You can also switch on Flag mode and flag with simple taps — perfect for phones.
  4. Reveal every safe tile to clear the level. Touch one mine and it's boom — game over. There are 100 levels: the board and the mine count grow as you climb, from 9×9 with 10 mines to 20×20 with 80. Clear fast for 3 stars — and beat your best time on every level!

Controls

  • Mouse: left-click to reveal a tile · right-click to plant or remove a flag.
  • Touch: tap to reveal · long-press (hold half a second) to flag · or turn on Flag mode and flag with plain taps.

The LessonThink before you act — one careless move can cost everything.

In Minesweeper, every click is a decision you can't take back. The numbers give you all the information you need — but only if you slow down and actually read them. The players who win aren't the fastest clickers; they're the ones who think one move ahead and only act when the logic is clear.

Life hands you the same board every day. Rushed choices — the angry reply, the skipped homework, the impulse buy — are clicks on unflagged tiles. Pause, count your numbers, and make the move you can defend. Careful beats fast, every single time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play Minesweeper?

Click or tap a tile to reveal it. The number on a tile tells you how many mines are touching it — use the numbers to work out where the mines hide, and reveal every safe tile to clear the level.

Can my first click ever hit a mine?

No. The first click is always safe, so you can start every level with a brave click.

How do I mark a tile where I think a mine is hiding?

Long-press the tile on a touch screen, or right-click with a mouse, to plant a flag. You can also switch on Flag mode and flag tiles with simple taps.

How many levels are there?

There are 100 levels. The board and the mine count grow as you climb, from 9×9 with 10 mines on level 1 to 20×20 with 80 mines on level 100.

What does Minesweeper teach?

It teaches you to think before you act. The numbers give you all the information you need, but only if you slow down and read them — careful beats fast, every time.
