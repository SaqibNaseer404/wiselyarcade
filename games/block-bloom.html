How to Play

If you love satisfying block puzzles, you'll feel right at home — three pieces at a time, one 8×8 board, and one golden rule: a full row or a full column bursts in a sweep of light and frees the space again.

  1. Drag a piece from the tray onto the board — or tap a piece, then tap where it should go. The ghost shows you exactly how it will land: green means it fits, red means it doesn't.
  2. Fill a complete row or column to clear it. Clear two or more lines with one piece for a big bonus — the points grow fast.
  3. When all three pieces are placed, a fresh set of three arrives. In Endless mode you play until no piece fits anywhere — chase your best score.
  4. In Level mode there are 100 levels. Each level gives you a target score (some also ask for a number of lines) and a fixed number of piece sets. Reach the target before the sets run out to clear the level — finish fast for 3 stars.
  5. The climb gets serious: higher levels start with pre-filled cells on the board and deal bigger, trickier pieces, while the targets keep rising. Your progress and stars save on your device.

Controls

  • Mouse: drag a piece onto the board, or click a piece then click a square to place it.
  • Touch: drag with your finger, or tap a piece, then tap the board.

The LessonPlan ahead — leave room for the future.

A grid filled in a rush has no space for the piece that matters; a life filled in a rush is the same. In Block Bloom, the players who survive aren't the fastest — they're the ones who glance at all three pieces before placing the first, and keep a corner free for the awkward shape they know is coming.

Place each piece where it helps the next one too. Leave room for the future — in the grid, and in your day.
