How to Play

  1. Guide the snake to the glowing red orb to eat it and grow longer.
  2. Every orb scores a point — and the snake gets a little faster. Eat the target number of orbs to clear the level and unlock the next of 100 levels.
  3. The game ends if you hit a wall, bite your own tail — or crash into a neon block (they appear from Level 11 and grow into mazes).
  4. Plan your path: a long snake needs room to turn!

Controls

  • Keyboard: ↑ ↓ ← → or WASD to steer. Space to start, P to pause.
  • Touch: swipe on the game to steer. Tap to start or resume.

The LessonGreed traps you — know when enough is enough

Snake feels great at first — every glowing orb makes you longer, stronger, hungrier. But the longer you get, the more dangerous your own tail becomes, until one greedy bite too many ends the run.

That's the trap of greed in real life too: chasing "just a little more" can tangle up everything you've already built. Enjoy the game, celebrate the growth — but know when enough is enough.
