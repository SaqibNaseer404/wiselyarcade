How to Play

Full classic chess against the computer — castling, en passant, and promotion all work. Pick your side and difficulty, then tap one of your pieces to see its legal moves (green dots), and tap a highlighted square to move.

  • Easy — a relaxed opponent that sometimes blunders. Great for learning.
  • Medium — thinks two moves ahead. A real challenge.
  • Hard — thinks three moves ahead. Bring your best.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap a piece, then tap a highlighted square. That's it.

The LessonEvery move has a consequence

In chess, you can't take a move back — the piece stays where you put it, and the whole board changes because of it. Life works the same way: every choice, big or small, moves something on your board.

So the habit chess builds is the habit winners use everywhere: pause, look ahead two or three moves, and then act. Think before you move — on the board, and off it.
