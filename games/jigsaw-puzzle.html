How to Play

  1. Look at the pale ghost picture on the board — that is the picture you are building.
  2. Drag a piece from the tray onto the board with your mouse or finger.
  3. Drop a piece near its true home and it snaps in with a pop and locks there.
  4. Stuck? Hold the 👁 Peek button to see the finished picture — but peeking costs you a star, so peek only when you truly need to!
  5. Finish the picture to earn stars and unlock the next level. There are 100 levels: six posters from your favourite Wisely Arcade games, and the pieces get smaller and trickier as you climb.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: press a piece, drag it, and let go to drop it.
  • Peek: press and hold the 👁 Peek button; let go to hide the picture again.

The LessonPatience — the picture comes together piece by piece

A jigsaw never gets solved by shaking the box. It happens quietly: one piece, then another, then another — and every piece you place makes the next one easier to find. Forcing a piece where it doesn't belong never works; looking carefully and waiting for the right fit always does.

That is true far beyond this board. Big homework, a new skill, even a friendship — they all come together piece by piece, not all at once. So when something feels too big, don't panic and don't rush. Pick up one small piece, put it where it belongs, and trust the picture to appear. Patience wins, every time.
