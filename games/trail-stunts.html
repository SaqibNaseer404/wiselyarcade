How to Play

  1. Hold ↑ (or W) to throttle over the hills and reach the checkered flag at the end of the trail.
  2. In the air, use ← / → (or A / D) to tilt the bike — lean back for a backflip, forward for a frontflip.
  3. Land on both wheels, fairly level, to score a flip bonus. Land nose-first or tail-first at speed and you'll wipe out!
  4. If your rider's head hits the ground, that's a wipeout too. Keep the rubber side down.
  5. Finish all 3 trails — each one hillier and longer than the last. Your best time per trail is saved.
  6. Love Moto X3M-style stunt riding? You'll feel at home here — Trail Stunts is our own original take on the physics bike-stunt genre.

Controls

  • Keyboard: ↑/W throttle · ↓/S brake · ←/A lean back · →/D lean forward · R restart.
  • Touch: use the on-screen buttons — GAS and BRAKE on the edges, LEAN buttons to tilt in the air.

The LessonBalance beats speed — control wins the race

Trail Stunts tempts you to hold the throttle down the whole way — and that's exactly how you wipe out. The riders who finish fastest aren't the ones going full speed everywhere; they're the ones who brake before the big jump, tilt gently in the air, and land level. Control beats raw speed, every trail.

Life rides the same way. Rushing every decision feels fast, but it ends in wipeouts — missed chances, sloppy work, burnout. The people who go farest aren't the fastest starters; they're the ones who balance ambition with self-control. Ease off the throttle when you need to, and you'll finish the race.
