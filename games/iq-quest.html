How to Play

  1. There are 100 levels. Level 1 starts with gentle picture patterns — and every level you climb adds something tougher: number sequences, letter codes, analogies, riddles, and finally Expert puzzle grids.
  2. Each level is 5 puzzles. Solve them all to unlock the next level — the map above shows your stars and what is still locked.
  3. After every correct answer, the game tells you why it was right — that is where the real learning happens.
  4. A star is earned when you solve a puzzle on your first try. Replay any unlocked level to win more stars!

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap or click an answer button.
  • Wrong answer? The button shakes and you can think again — no timer, no punishment.

The LessonSmart is not magic — it's noticing patterns

Nobody is born knowing the answer. Smart people just look longer. They notice that the numbers grow by 3 each time, that one picture doesn't belong, that the same trick keeps repeating. IQ is not a gift you either have or don't — it is a habit of paying attention, and habits can be trained, one puzzle at a time.

Life is full of patterns too. The friend who always shows up. The habit that always costs you money at month's end. The small choice that always leads somewhere good. Learn to spot patterns in puzzles, and you start spotting them in life — and that is when you start making wiser moves.
