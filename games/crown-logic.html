How to Play

The board is split into coloured regions — one region for every row. Your job is to place crowns so that four rules all hold at once.

  1. One crown in every row and one in every column.
  2. One crown in every colour region, however tangled its shape.
  3. No two crowns may touch — not side by side, and not even diagonally at the corners.
  4. Tap a square to place a crown. If it breaks a rule, both crowns flash and it costs you a heart — you get 3 hearts per level, and three mistakes end the level. Tap a crown again to turn it into an X — a free note meaning "no crown here". Tap the X once more to clear it. X notes are unlimited thinking aids; they never cost hearts.
  5. Stars: finish with no mistakes for 3 stars, one mistake for 2 stars, two mistakes for 1 star.
  6. There are 100 levels. The board grows from 6×6 to 10×10 and the regions twist into trickier shapes as you climb. Every single board is solver-verified to have exactly one solution — if you are stuck, the answer is always hiding in the rules, never in a guess. Your progress and stars save on your device.

Controls

  • Mouse: click a square to cycle it — empty → crown → X note → empty.
  • Touch: tap a square the same way. Use X notes to cross out squares the rules rule out, and the crowns will find themselves.

The LessonEvery constraint is a clue.

Limits don't block you — they quietly tell you where the answer must be. In Crown Logic, every "no" is information: a full row crosses out a column, a crowded colour corners its crown, and the last empty square in a region is a crown whether you feel ready or not.

Life's hard problems work the same way. The budget, the deadline, the rule you wish wasn't there — each one deletes a hundred wrong answers and points at the few that remain. Read the limits like clues, and the path appears.
