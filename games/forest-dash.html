How to Play

If you love Subway Surfers and Temple Run, you'll feel right at home — but this time the forest is real 3D. Sprint down three lanes through pine trees toward the great mountain on the horizon, dodge what blocks you, grab every glowing firefly, and see how far the journey takes you.

  1. You run forward automatically and the trail keeps getting faster. Switch lanes with ← →, jump with ↑, slide with ↓.
  2. Fallen logs — jump clean over them. Low branches — slide right under. Rock walls — you can't jump or slide through these, so switch lanes!
  3. Grab glowing fireflies for bonus points. Reach the mountain to complete the level — each new level is a deeper, darker, faster forest, from Sunny Grove to Thunder Forest and beyond. How many peaks can you conquer?
  4. One hit ends the run. Watch ahead, plan your moves, and chase your best score. How far can you go?

Controls

  • Keyboard: ← → or A D to switch lanes · ↑ / W / Space to jump · ↓ / S to slide.
  • Touch: swipe left/right to switch lanes, swipe up to jump, swipe down to slide, tap to jump.

The LessonKeep going — every obstacle passes

In Forest Dash, every log, branch, and rock wall looks like the end of the run — until you jump it, slide under it, or sidestep it, and it's behind you in a second. That's the whole game: obstacles only stop you if you stop moving.

Life works the same way. Bad weeks, hard exams, rejections — they feel permanent while you're staring at them, but they pass the way every obstacle on the trail passes: you keep going. You don't have to be the fastest runner. You just have to not stop.
