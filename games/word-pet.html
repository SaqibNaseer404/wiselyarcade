How to Play

  1. Look at the picture — that is the word Biscuit is thinking of.
  2. Tap the letter tiles in order to spell the whole word. Each letter you place fills a tile above.
  3. Every finished word feeds Biscuit and plays with him — watch him hop, spin, and grow a little with every word.
  4. There are 100 levels, with 6 words in each. Words start tiny (CAT, DOG) and grow longer as you climb. From Level 21, trap letters sneak into the tiles; from Level 51 the picture hides until you place a letter; and from Level 81 the word is a complete mystery until you finish it.
  5. Biscuit grows through the stages — Egg, Baby, Pup, Young, Big — and at Level 100 he becomes a Champion with a golden crown. Every 10 levels, a new pet friend joins your My Pets collection.
  6. A star is earned for each word you spell with no mistakes — how many can you collect?

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap or click the letter tiles, in the order they appear in the word.
  • Wrong letter? No problem — the tile shakes, and you can try again. Trying is how we learn.

The LessonLove and care help everything grow

Biscuit does not grow in one giant jump. He grows word by word — a little food, a little play, a little love, every single day. That is how puppies grow, how gardens grow, how friendships grow… and how readers grow, too.

Nothing you care for grows all at once, and nothing grows if it is ignored. Small care, given often, is what makes big, strong, happy things. So whatever you are growing — a pet, a plant, a skill, a friendship — feed it a little today. Then again tomorrow. One day you will look back and see how far it has come.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play Word Pet?

Look at the picture — that is the word Biscuit is thinking of. Tap the shuffled letter tiles in order to spell the whole word, and every finished word feeds Biscuit and helps him grow.

How many levels are there?

There are 100 levels, with 6 words in each level. Words start tiny, like CAT and DOG, and grow longer as you climb — all the way to champion words like STRAWBERRY and HELICOPTER.

What happens if I tap the wrong letter?

No problem — the tile shakes and you can try again. A star is earned for each word you spell with no mistakes.

How does Biscuit grow?

Biscuit grows a little with every finished word, and his stage grows as you climb the levels — Egg, Baby, Pup, Young, Big, and Champion with a golden crown at level 100. Every 10 levels, a new pet friend joins your collection.

What does Word Pet teach?

It teaches that love and care help everything grow. Nothing grows all at once — small care, given often, is what makes big, strong, happy things.
