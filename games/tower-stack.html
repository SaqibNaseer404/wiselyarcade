How to Play

  1. A block slides back and forth above your tower. Tap, click, or press Space to drop it.
  2. Any part hanging over the edge gets sliced off — the tower keeps only what lands squarely.
  3. Land a block almost perfectly and it stays whole. Stack them true to earn a Perfect streak.
  4. Every 5 blocks is a new level: the block slides faster, and from level 3 the wind starts pushing it.
  5. Miss the tower completely and the block tumbles away — game over. How high can you build?

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap or click anywhere on the tower to drop the block.
  • Keyboard: Space or ↓ to drop the block.

The LessonSteady wins — rushing topples towers

Every block in Tower Stack is a small decision, and hurried decisions make leaning towers. The players who build highest aren't the fastest tappers — they're the ones who wait half a second for the right moment, every single time.

Life stacks the same way. Skills, friendships, a career — they're all built one careful block at a time, and no one remembers how fast you built, only whether it stood. Slow down, aim true, and let steady win.
