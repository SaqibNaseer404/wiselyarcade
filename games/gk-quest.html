How to Play

  1. There are 100 levels. Every level mixes categories — 🌍 world, 🦁 animals, 🔬 science, 🚀 space, 🏛️ history, ⚽ sports, 🍎 food — and the facts get tougher the higher you climb.
  2. Each level is 5 questions. Answer them all to unlock the next level — the map above shows your stars and what is still locked.
  3. After every correct answer, the game shares the true fact behind it — that is where the real learning happens.
  4. A star is earned when you answer on your first try. Replay any unlocked level to win more stars!

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap or click an answer button.
  • Wrong answer? The button shakes and you can think again — no timer, no punishment.

The LessonKnowing a little about everything opens every door

General knowledge looks like trivia, but it is really a map of the world. Every fact you pick up — how bees make honey, why Mars is red, who first stepped on the Moon — becomes a hook that the next fact can hang on. Curious people learn faster, not because they are smarter, but because they have more hooks.

So ask questions about everything. Wonder why the sky is blue and who built the pyramids. The world rewards the curious — one fact at a time.
