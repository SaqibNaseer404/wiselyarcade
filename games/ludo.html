How to Play

Classic Ludo against three computer players. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your base, race all four tokens around the board and up your home stretch. Land on an opponent (off a star square) to send them back to base. Rolling 6, capturing, or bringing a token home earns another roll — but three 6s in a row skips your turn!

  • ☆ star squares are safe — nobody can capture you there.
  • You need the exact roll to enter home.
  • Tap a glowing token to move it.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap the dice button, then tap a glowing token.

The LessonYou can't control the dice

In Ludo, nobody chooses their roll — the dice do what the dice do. Winners aren't the luckiest players; they're the ones who make the smartest move with whatever number they got.

Life rolls its own dice too: surprises, setbacks, lucky breaks. You can't control the roll — but you always control how you play it. Play your number well.
