How to Play

  1. Look at the two pictures. They look the same — but the right-hand picture is hiding some differences!
  2. When you spot one, tap it — on either picture. A green ring appears on both pictures and your Found counter goes up.
  3. There are 100 levels and five pictures: the Rocket, the Castle, Under the Sea, the Friendly Robot and the Garden. Higher levels hide more differences — 5 at first, then 6, 7, 8, and 10 in the last levels!
  4. Find every difference to finish the level. Stars: finish with no mistakes for 3 stars. Tapping a wrong spot is okay — it just counts as a mistake, so slow down and look carefully.
  5. Stuck? Press 💡 Hint and a golden ring shows you where one difference is hiding — but a hint means the level can earn at most 2 stars.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap or click a difference on either picture.
  • No timer pressure: the clock only counts up, so take as long as you like. Careful eyes beat fast fingers!

The LessonSlow down and really look

The differences in this game are never hidden by magic — they are hiding in plain sight. The moon that vanished, the flag that changed colour… they were there all along, waiting for someone patient enough to notice.

That is true far beyond this game. Careful eyes find what rushing misses: the small print, the friend who looks sad, the one wrong number in your homework. Slow down, look twice, and trust what you see. Your attention is a superpower — use it slowly.
