How to Play

The grove is full of glossy gems — ruby circles, sapphire diamonds, aqua teardrops and more. Your job: swap, match, and chain your way to each level's target before your moves run out.

  1. Swap two neighbouring gems — tap one, then tap the gem next to it, or drag one into the other. The swap only counts if it makes a match of 3 or more; otherwise the gems swap back. Every good swap costs 1 move.
  2. Matched gems pop, gems above fall into the gaps, and fresh gems rain in from the top. If the fall makes another match, the cascade multiplies your points — ×2, ×3 and beyond.
  3. Match 4 in a row to grow a line gem: match it later and it blasts its whole row or column. Match 5 in a row to grow a star gem: swap it with any gem and every gem of that colour vanishes.
  4. There are 100 levels. Each level sets a score target and a move limit, and both get tougher as you climb — new gem types join the grove at levels 26 and 61. The grove itself changes season every 10 levels.
  5. Stars: reaching the target earns 1 star, scoring 120% of the target earns 2 stars, and 150% earns 3 stars. Run out of moves below the target and you simply try the level again — no dead ends, ever.
  6. If the board ever runs out of possible matches, the grove reshuffles the gems for you, so a level can always be finished. Your progress and stars save on your device.

Controls

  • Mouse: click a gem, then click a neighbour to swap — or drag one gem into its neighbour.
  • Touch: tap two neighbouring gems, or drag with your finger.

The LessonSmall moves add up — one good match sets off a chain.

Nobody clears a level in one giant swap. In Gem Grove Quest you win the way the grove grows: one small, well-chosen move at a time — and every so often, one good move sets off a whole cascade you never saw coming.

Life works the same way. One good habit triggers the next: the short walk leads to the better sleep, the better sleep leads to the clearer morning. Don't wait for a huge change. Make the small move in front of you — and let the chain begin.
