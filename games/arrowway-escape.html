How to Play

Every tile on the tray is an arrow pointing up, down, left or right. An arrow can only leave the tray by sliding straight in the direction it points — and only when nothing stands between it and the edge.

  1. Tap an arrow. If every square between it and the edge is empty, it slides off the tray and is gone. If another arrow — or a steel block — is in the way, it bumps, stays put… and costs you a heart.
  2. You get 3 hearts per level. Three blocked taps and the level is over — so look before you tap.
  3. Clear every arrow off the tray to finish the level. Each escape frees the arrows that were trapped behind it, so the right order unlocks the whole board.
  4. Stars: clear a level with no mistakes for 3 stars, one mistake for 2 stars, two mistakes for 1 star.
  5. There are 100 levels. The tray grows from a friendly 4×4 to a packed 10×10, the arrows pack in tighter, and from level 51 on, riveted steel blocks join the tray as fixed obstacles. Your progress and stars save on your device.

Controls

  • Mouse: click an arrow to try to free it.
  • Touch: tap an arrow. The tile colours match their direction — amber up, blue right, green down, pink left — so you can read the tray at a glance.

The LessonOrder matters.

Free the blocked pieces — and problems — in the right sequence instead of forcing the first one you see. In Arrowway Escape, the arrow you want most is usually trapped behind two others; the win comes from finding the one that can move now and letting each escape unlock the next.

Life works the same way. When everything feels stuck, don't push harder on the blocked piece — look for the one small thing that can move first, and let the path open up.
