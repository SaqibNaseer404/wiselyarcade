How to Play

  1. Pick your level: Toddler counts apples up to 5, Kids learns the four kinds of sums, Teen takes on times tables to 12, Adults meets 3-digit sums and missing numbers, and Expert solves two-step problems.
  2. Pick a kind of sum — ➕ add, ➖ subtract, ✖️ multiply, ➗ divide — or 🔀 Mixed for a bit of everything.
  3. Read the sum and tap the right answer from the buttons.
  4. A star is earned when you answer on your first try. Finish 10 questions (8 for Toddler) and see your score!

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap or click an answer button.
  • Wrong answer? The button shakes and you can try again — no points lost, no timer chasing you.

The LessonBig problems break into small steps

A sum like 48 × 6 looks scary — until you break it down: 48 × 6 is 40 × 6 plus 8 × 6. Two small steps you already know, and suddenly the "hard" problem is done. That is the secret of every level in this game: nothing here is one big jump, it is always small steps stacked up.

Life works the same way. Learning an instrument, saving money, building a business, getting fit — none of it happens in one leap. It happens one small, honest step at a time. So don't fear the big problem. Find its first small step, take it, and keep climbing — level by level, just like this quest.
