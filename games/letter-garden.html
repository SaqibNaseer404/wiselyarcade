How to Play

  1. Look at the big picture. What word is it?
  2. Tap the letter tiles below in order to build the whole word — first letter, then the next, then the next.
  3. Every correct letter drops water and sunshine on your seed. Finish the word and your plant grows one stage: seed → sprout → stem → leaves → buds → full bloom!
  4. Each level is 6 words and grows one whole plant. Finished plants move into My Garden at the top — finish a level to unlock the next.
  5. There are 100 levels. Words start tiny (SUN, BEE) and grow longer as you climb. From Level 21, two trick tiles hide among the letters. From Level 51, the picture hides until you place the first letter — and at the top levels, the picture is your reward at the end. Level 100 grows the one-and-only Golden Tree 🌟🌳.
  6. A star is earned for every word you complete on your first try — how many can you collect?

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap or click the letter tiles, in order, to build the word.
  • Wrong letter? No problem — the tile shakes, and you can try again. Trying is how gardens — and readers — grow.

The LessonSmall steps every day make big things grow

Nobody plants a seed in the morning and picks flowers by lunch. A garden grows a little bit every day — a drop of water here, a ray of sunshine there — until one day you look up and the whole garden is in bloom. This game works the same way: one letter, then one word, then one plant at a time.

That is how every big thing in life grows. You do not need a giant leap. You need one small step today, and another small step tomorrow. Keep stepping, keep watering, and soon you'll look back at a garden you grew yourself — and wonder why it ever felt so far away.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play Letter Garden?

Look at the big picture, then tap the shuffled letter tiles in order to complete the word. Every finished word grows your plant one stage — seed, sprout, stem, leaves, buds, full bloom. Six words grow one whole plant.

How many levels are there?

There are 100 levels, with 6 words in each level. Words start tiny, like SUN and BEE, and grow longer as you climb — up to long words of 9 to 12 letters at the top. From level 21, two trick tiles hide among the letters.

What happens if I tap the wrong letter?

No problem — the tile shakes and you can try again. There is no punishment. A star is earned for every word you complete on your first try.

What is the Golden Tree?

The Golden Tree is the special plant that grows on level 100 — the last level. Only players who grow all 100 plants earn it, and it takes its place at the end of your garden.

What does Letter Garden teach?

It teaches that small steps every day make big things grow. One letter, then one word, then one plant at a time — exactly how real gardens, and real learning, grow.
