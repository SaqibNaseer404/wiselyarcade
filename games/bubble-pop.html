How to Play

If you love classic bubble shooters, you'll feel right at home — but these glossy 3D bubbles pop more satisfyingly than any you've seen. Line up the cannon, plan each shot, and clear the board before it reaches you.

  1. Move your mouse or drag your finger to aim the cannon — a dotted guide shows exactly where your bubble will fly, including bounces off the walls.
  2. Click or release to shoot. Match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to pop them in a juicy burst.
  3. Bubbles left hanging with nothing connecting them to the top tumble down for bonus points — set up big drops by popping their anchors!
  4. Every 6 shots, a new row slides in from the top. If any bubble crosses the red danger line, the game is over.
  5. Clear the whole board to beat your campaign level — there are 100 levels, and higher ones start with more rows and a sneaky sixth color. Clear in 14 shots or fewer for 3 stars! Then keep popping — the boards keep coming. How far can you pop?

Controls

  • Mouse: move to aim, click to shoot.
  • Touch: drag to aim, release to shoot.

The LessonAim with intention — random shots waste good chances.

In Bubble Pop, every bubble you fire sticks around. Spray shots randomly and the board fills up, the danger line creeps closer, and suddenly you're out of moves. But line up each shot — pick the color, plan the bounce, pop a whole cluster — and the board melts away.

Life works the same way. Random effort fills your days without moving you forward; intentional effort clears the path. Aim with intention: choose where your energy goes, and make every shot count.
