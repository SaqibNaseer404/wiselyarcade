How to Play

  1. Tap a colour in the palette below the picture.
  2. Tap any part of the picture — the rocket, the planets, the stars, even the sky — to paint it.
  3. There are 100 levels and five pictures: the Rocket (Levels 1–20), the Castle (21–40), Under the Sea (41–60), the Friendly Robot (61–80) and the Garden (81–100). Inside every picture, new details appear every 4 levels, and new colours unlock every 10 levels. Finish a picture to unlock the next level.
  4. Paint every part you can see. Free colours are yours to choose — but from Level 6, the 🎯 Colour Mission names a few parts and colours: match them all to earn 3 stars!
  5. Press the big finish button — Launch, Raise the flag, Open the treasure, Switch on or Bloom — and celebrate your finished picture!

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: tap a colour, then tap a part of the picture.
  • Made a mistake? Just tap the part again with a different colour.

The LessonYour ideas can fly

Every rocket on this page starts plain white. It only becomes special when someone dares to add their own colours — red nose, purple wings, a green sky, why not? Creativity means your way of seeing things is worth showing.

That is true far beyond this picture. The drawing you make, the story you tell, the idea you share in class — nobody else in the world sees things exactly the way you do. Don't copy someone else's rocket. Paint yours, launch it proudly, and remember: your ideas can fly.
