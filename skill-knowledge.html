About knowledge & creativity games

Curiosity is a skill too. GK Quest asks 100 levels of questions about animals, space, science, geography, history, sports and food — and every answer teaches a true fact, right or wrong. IQ Quest stretches the same muscle with patterns and riddles. And for the youngest visitors, Paint the Rocket hands over the colours and steps back: five big pictures, no wrong answers, and ideas that can fly.

Good to know

Is it free? Do I need an account?
100% free. No account, no download — open a game and play in your browser, on phone, tablet or computer.

Are the age labels strict?
No — they are guidance. Children develop differently, so parents should pick what fits their child. Every game here also appears in its age hub: Kids (5–9) · Teens (10–17) · Adults (18+).
